# Privacy Policy for Tab Rat

_Last updated: August 23, 2026_

Tab Rat ("the app," "we," "us") explains what data it collects, how it's used, and your choices below.

## Data we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account, we collect your email address and password to authenticate you. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and are never stored or visible to us in plain text.

**App content.** Trips, homes, expenses, friends, groups, and settlement records you create are stored under your account so they sync across your devices. This data is only accessible to you — it's never shared with other users or third parties.

**Profile information (optional).** If you set a display name or profile photo, it's stored under your account.

**Receipt images (optional).** If you use the receipt-scanning feature, the photo you take is sent directly to Anthropic's API to extract item and price data. We do not store or retain these images ourselves.

**Anthropic API key (optional).** Receipt scanning requires you to provide your own Anthropic API key. This key is stored only on your device (in the operating system's secure credential storage, or your browser session on web) — we never see it, transmit it to our servers, or store it ourselves.

## Data we don't collect

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Third-party services

- **Supabase** stores your account and app data. Access is restricted to your own account via database-level security rules — no one else, including us, can query another user's data through the app.
- **Anthropic** processes receipt images only if you use the scanning feature, using an API key you provide yourself.

## Data deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and all app data immediately.

## Children's privacy

Tab Rat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
