# Privacy Policy for Tab Rat

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

Tab Rat ("the app," "we," "us") explains what data it collects, how it's used, and your choices below.

## Data we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account, we collect your email address and password to authenticate you. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and are never stored or visible to us in plain text. If you sign in with Apple or Google instead, we receive your email address from that provider.

**App content.** Trips, homes, expenses, friends, groups, and settlement records you create are stored under your account so they sync across your devices. This data is private to you unless you choose to share a trip, home, or bill using its join code. Anyone who joins with that code can view and edit that trip, home, or bill and its expenses. We never share your data with third parties for marketing.

**Profile information (optional).** If you set a display name, username, or profile photo, it's stored under your account. People you share a trip, home, or bill with can see your display name and username.

**Receipt images (optional).** If you use the receipt-scanning feature, the app asks for your permission first, then sends the photo through our server to Claude, an AI service from Anthropic, to read the items and prices. We do not store or retain these images ourselves.

## Data we don't collect

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Third-party services

- **Supabase** stores your account and app data. Access is restricted to your own account via database-level security rules — no one else, including us, can query another user's data through the app.
- **Anthropic** (Claude AI) processes receipt images only if you use the scanning feature. Anthropic handles that data under its own privacy policy.
- **Apple** and **Google** handle sign-in if you choose to sign in with them.

## Data deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and all app data immediately.

## Children's privacy

Tab Rat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
